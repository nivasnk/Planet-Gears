About TORQLINE

Three Decades of Engineered Motion

Founded in 1990 as a two-machine job shop, TORQLINE is today a vertically integrated gear manufacturer with 120,000 m² of production space, 400+ specialists and customers on six continents.

We partner with OEMs and plant operators from first calculation to final inspection — reverse-engineering legacy gears, optimising tooth geometry for noise and load, and delivering certified parts with full material traceability.

  • Design & EngineeringTooth-contact analysis, FEA and reverse engineering.
  • Integrated PlantsForging, machining, heat treatment and grinding in-house.
  • Global LogisticsExport packing and door-to-door delivery to 40+ countries.
  • Lifecycle SupportBreakdown replacements and gearbox refurbishment.

Manufacturing Capabilities

From Billet to Certified Gear

A fully controlled, in-house process chain means shorter lead times, tighter tolerances and complete traceability.

  2. STEP 01

    Design

    Load, life and tooth-contact calculation to ISO 6336 / AGMA 2001.

  3. STEP 02

    Forging

    Closed-die and ring-rolled blanks from certified alloy steel.

  4. STEP 03

    Hobbing

    CNC hobbing, shaping and skiving up to module 30.

  5. STEP 04

    Heat Treat

    Gas carburizing, nitriding and induction hardening in-house.

  6. STEP 05

    Grinding

    Profile & generating grinding to DIN 5 with topological modification.

  7. STEP 06

    Inspection

    CMM, gear analyzers and MPI with full inspection reports.
